# Gatehouse customer-facing site

## Decisions from the November 11, 2026 UX review

- The homepage sends visitors to the quote calculator. It has no price table, starting prices or tier guide. Its payment disclosure still shows $0 due today.
- The homepage links to the room checklist, FAQ and service areas instead of duplicating their full content.
- Service cards describe each service and link to its quote. The comparison remains on Services, and the detailed checklist remains on What's Included.
- The quote form omits optional square footage, extra room checkboxes and SMS opt-in. The customer can describe relevant details in visit notes.
- Half bathrooms (owner decision 13.11.2026, supersedes the 11.11 omission): a "Half bathrooms" selector (0–2) sits next to "Full bathrooms" on step 1. It never changes the price — tier and price use only bedrooms + full bathrooms — and the count flows to the cleaner checklist as "Half bathroom" rooms (no tub/shower items).
- City pages keep a visit summary and local information. About keeps the owner introduction and service expectations.
